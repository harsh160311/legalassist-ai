General Legal Information

This section provides general information about common legal concepts and document types. This information is for educational purposes only and should not be considered legal advice.

Common Legal Document Types

Contracts

A binding agreement between two or more parties that creates mutual obligations. Contracts typically include offer, acceptance, consideration, and mutual assent.

Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDA)

A contract that establishes a confidential relationship between parties. NDAs protect sensitive information from being shared with third parties.

Employment Agreements

A contract between an employer and employee that outlines the terms of employment, including compensation, duties, and termination conditions.

Lease Agreements

A contract between a landlord and tenant that outlines the terms of renting property, including rent, duration, and maintenance responsibilities.

Terms of Service (ToS)

A legal agreement between a service provider and user that governs the use of a service or product.

Privacy Policy

A document that explains how an organization collects, uses, and protects user data.

Indian Legal Aid & Free Resources

National Legal Services Authority (NALSA)

The apex body for legal aid in India, established under the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987. Provides free legal services to eligible persons including women, children, SC/ST members, industrial workers, and persons in custody.

Website: nalsa.gov.in

District Legal Services Authorities (DLSA)

Each district has a DLSA that organizes legal aid camps, provides lawyer assistance, and runs Lok Adalats (people's courts) for amicable dispute resolution at the district level.

Legal Aid under Section 304 CrPC

Under Section 304 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, every person accused of an offence (including offences punishable with death) is entitled to free legal services at the state's expense if they cannot afford a lawyer.

Online Legal Aid Portal

Apply for legal aid online through the government's portal for e-filing and tracking of legal aid applications.

Portal: olas.gov.in/olas

Important Helplines

  • Women Helpline: 181 (24/7, free for women in distress)
  • Consumer Helpline: 1800-11-4000 (toll-free, for consumer grievances)
  • Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930 (for reporting online fraud and cyber crimes)
  • Police Emergency: 100
  • Legal Aid Helpline: 15160 (NALSA's legal aid helpline)

Lok Adalats (People's Courts)

Free and speedier dispute resolution forums organized under the Legal Services Authorities Act. Awards passed by Lok Adalats are binding and have the status of a civil court decree. No court fee is required.

Key Legal Concepts

Liability

Legal responsibility for one's acts or omissions. Limited liability caps the amount one party can be held responsible for.

Indemnification

A contractual obligation where one party agrees to compensate another for certain costs or damages.

Jurisdiction

The authority of a court to hear and decide a case. Governing law determines which jurisdiction's laws apply to a contract.

Breach of Contract

When one party fails to fulfill their obligations under a contract, potentially allowing the other party to seek remedies.

Force Majeure

Clauses that excuse parties from liability when extraordinary events beyond their control prevent performance.

Severability

A clause that allows the rest of a contract to remain valid if one provision is found unenforceable.

When to Consult a Lawyer

While AI tools can help you understand legal documents, you should consult a qualified attorney when:

  • The stakes are high (significant money, property, or rights)
  • You're signing a complex agreement
  • You need personalized legal advice
  • You're involved in a dispute or litigation
  • You need to understand your specific legal rights
  • The document involves multiple jurisdictions
  • You're unsure about any terms or implications

India-Specific Scenarios Requiring Legal Counsel

  • Property Disputes & Registration: Title verification, encumbrance checks, sale deed drafting, mutation of land records, and disputes under the Transfer of Property Act, 1882
  • Family Matters: Divorce proceedings under the Hindu Marriage Act or Special Marriage Act, child custody under the Guardians and Wards Act, maintenance claims under Section 125 CrPC or the Hindu Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act
  • Criminal Charges: Defence under the Indian Penal Code / Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, bail applications, and navigating the criminal justice system
  • Government Service Matters: Service disputes, writ petitions under Articles 226/32 of the Constitution, and RTI-related matters
  • Consumer Disputes: Complaints under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 before District/State/National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commissions
  • Labour & Employment: Disputes under industrial law, Payment of Wages Act, or ESIC/EPF matters
  • Tax & GST Matters: Appeals before Income Tax Appellate Tribunal (ITAT), GST Appellate Tribunal, or High Courts

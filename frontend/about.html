About LegalAssist AI

Our Mission

LegalAssist AI aims to make legal information more accessible by helping users understand, compare, and navigate legal documents. We believe that everyone deserves to understand the legal documents they sign.

What We Do

LegalAssist AI is an AI-powered platform that helps you understand complex legal documents. Simply upload your document and our AI will analyze it, explain key terms, identify risks, and answer your questions — all in plain language.

Document Analysis

Upload any legal document and get a comprehensive analysis including:

  • Simple-language summary
  • Document type detection
  • Key parties and obligations
  • Risk assessment
  • Action checklist

Document Comparison

Compare two versions of a document to identify:

  • Changed clauses
  • Added provisions
  • Removed terms
  • Modified obligations
  • Key differences

AI Q&A

Ask questions about your documents and get answers based only on the document content. Understand your rights and obligations without needing a law degree.

How It Works

1. Upload

Upload your legal document (PDF, DOCX, or TXT)

2. AI Analysis

Our AI reads and understands your document

3. Get Insights

Receive clear summary, risks, and recommendations

Technology

LegalAssist AI is built using:

  • FastAPI - Modern Python web framework
  • Google Gemini AI - Advanced language model for document analysis
  • Document Processing - PDF, DOCX, and text extraction
  • Prompt Safety - Protection against injection attacks

Safety & Privacy

Document Security

Documents are processed securely and are not stored permanently. We treat all uploaded documents as untrusted data.

Prompt Injection Protection

Our system includes safeguards against prompt injection attacks to ensure AI responses are safe and accurate.

No Legal Advice

We never claim to provide legal advice. Our outputs are informational only and should not be relied upon for legal decisions.

Important Disclaimer

This platform provides legal information and document assistance for informational purposes only.

It does not provide legal advice and does not replace a qualified legal professional.

Always consult with a qualified attorney for legal advice specific to your situation. Do not rely solely on AI-generated analysis for important legal decisions.
